# Dashboard product context

## Register

product

## Users

The local AI Office operator checks project work between commands: task details,
who is handling a stage or run, what needs attention, and recorded progress.

## Product Purpose

A read-only operations console backed by the authoritative Runtime query
surface. Help the operator move from project summaries to the evidence behind
a task or run without inventing assignments, completion, or execution authority.

## Brand Personality

Sober, precise, practical. Preserve the existing console's language and familiar
navigation while making details easier to discover.

## Anti-references

Decorative metrics, invented health scores, charts computed from partial samples,
and role labels presented as proof of authority do not fit this console.

## Design Principles

- Let every task title lead to its own detail.
- Explain the difference between a stage assignment and a run's agent.
- Use exact counts and disclose presentation limits beside the evidence.
- Keep recorded status, operational status, and requirement progress distinct.
- Preserve the read-only boundary and existing live synchronization.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native links and labelled controls, visible keyboard focus, textual chart
values, and structural layouts that work on narrow screens. Respect the user's
system color scheme and avoid unnecessary motion.
